Compact Two-Bedroom Home Plan

A single-storey concept that brings the shared living spaces together while keeping the bedrooms in a quieter zone.

2 bedrooms · 1 bathroom · single storey

Design intention: This concept is intended as a starting point for a small household that wants two private bedrooms, a shared living area and a straightforward connection between the kitchen and dining space.

Suggested room schedule

SpaceIllustrative sizePlanning note
Primary bedroom12 × 13 ftAllow room for bed access and wardrobe doors.
Bedroom 210 × 11 ftCan serve as a bedroom, study or flexible guest room.
Living and dining15 × 18 ftKeep a clear route between the entry, seating and dining zones.
Kitchen10 × 11 ftPlan counter space, ventilation and safe appliance clearances.
Bathroom8 × 6 ftCheck fixture clearances and local accessibility requirements.
Laundry6 × 6 ftConfirm appliance dimensions, drainage and ventilation.

Room sizes are illustrative clear-space targets, not measured dimensions from the diagram. Wall thickness, closets, circulation, structure and local requirements affect the final floor area.

Why the spaces are arranged this way

  • Shared spaces together: The living, dining and kitchen zones are close to support everyday family activities.
  • Bedrooms grouped: Keeping sleeping rooms away from the busiest part of the home can help create a quieter retreat.
  • Central access: A simple hall can make the bedrooms and bathroom easier to reach without walking through another private room.
  • Useful utility space: A dedicated laundry area helps keep washing and cleaning equipment out of the main living zone.

Questions to answer before adapting the concept

  1. Which direction does the site face, and where will the best daylight come from?
  2. Where are the road access, neighboring windows, drainage and service connections?
  3. Would the household benefit more from a larger kitchen, extra storage or a second bathroom?
  4. Do local planning rules set minimum room sizes, setbacks, ventilation or accessibility requirements?
  5. Can the structure, roof form and window locations be designed for the local climate and budget?
Important: This is an original concept for general inspiration, not a construction-ready architectural or engineering drawing. It does not show structural members, foundations, electrical or plumbing layouts, exact wall thicknesses, door swings or code-compliant dimensions. A qualified local architect, designer and relevant engineers must adapt and verify any plan for the actual site and jurisdiction before construction.
