Luxury House Plan 10: From Visual Concept to a Buildable House Plan

Use this design presentation to discuss priorities with an architect before developing measured construction drawings.

This illustration shows a two-storey villa with a glass balcony, a swimming pool and a contemporary brown tiled roof. The ground-floor plan labels several bedrooms and a kitchen/dining zone, while the cutaway suggests additional bedrooms on the upper level.

Image review: The title states 35 ft × 45 ft and five bedrooms, but only part of the floor-plan information is provided. The 2D panel is labelled “ground floor plan” and shows fewer bedrooms than the cutaway's overall five-bedroom arrangement. No separate upper-floor plan is supplied, so the full room count, stair connection and bathroom schedule cannot be verified. The 15 ft × 25 ft pool and the house footprint also need to be checked against a complete site plan.

Ground-floor and upper-floor planning

Request two separate, dimensioned floor plans. The ground floor should clearly identify the entrance, shared living/dining space, kitchen, guest or family bedroom, bathrooms and stairs. The upper floor should identify the remaining bedrooms, master suite, bathrooms, balcony access and circulation. Number every room once and provide a matching room schedule.

Pool and outdoor circulation

The pool is a major feature and should be located only after the building footprint, setbacks and access routes are confirmed. Include pool structure, waterproofing, filtration, pump location, drainage, non-slip paving and safety barriers. Ensure that the pool does not block access for maintenance or emergency movement.

Balcony, roof and structure

The glass balcony needs engineered supports and suitable safety glazing. The tiled roof requires a roof plan, framing design, gutters and rainwater disposal. A two-storey building also needs structural calculations for foundations, columns, beams, slabs and stairs based on site conditions.

Area, cost and approvals

Do not use 35 ft × 45 ft as confirmed total built-up area. State the plot size separately from the ground-floor area, upper-floor area, balcony and pool. Prepare a cost estimate from verified quantities and local rates, with currency, date, finishes and exclusions. Check planning approvals and pool requirements before work begins.

  • Complete ground- and upper-floor plans
  • Verified five-bedroom and bathroom schedule
  • Measured pool, setbacks and site access
  • Balcony, roof and structural engineering details
  • Coordinated services, approvals and bill of quantities
