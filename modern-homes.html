What makes a modern home practical?

Modern design is more than a flat roof, large windows or a minimalist façade. A useful home responds to its climate, plot shape, family routines and maintenance needs. Shaded openings, clear circulation, appropriately sized storage and well-coordinated services often matter more than decorative features.

How to use these concepts

Start with the site and room requirements, then compare the 2D arrangement with furniture footprints and a 3D view. Check daylight, privacy, ventilation, roof drainage and service access before choosing finishes. Ask qualified local professionals to adapt each concept to the actual land and applicable rules.
