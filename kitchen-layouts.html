Room Layout Ideas

Kitchen Layouts

Explore compact L-shaped, galley and U-shaped kitchen layouts with practical work zones, appliance clearances and ventilation guidance.

Practical kitchen shapes and work zones

Choose a layout around the room dimensions, entry, windows, ventilation and the positions of water, drainage and electrical services.

Concept 01 · 8 × 10 ft

L-Shaped Compact Kitchen

Two connected counter runs create an efficient work triangle and leave the opposite side open for circulation or a small dining ledge.

  • Keep sink, hob and refrigerator in a practical sequence.
  • Provide landing space beside the hob and sink.
  • Use upper cabinets without blocking windows or ventilation.
Concept 02 · 8 × 10 ft

Galley Kitchen

Parallel counters provide generous worktop length in a narrow room. The center aisle should be sized to suit the household and appliance doors.

  • Check dishwasher, oven and refrigerator door clearances.
  • Keep the main passage free of freestanding storage.
  • Plan task lighting over both counter runs.
Concept 03 · 9 × 10 ft

U-Shaped Work Kitchen

Three counter runs maximize storage and preparation surface. This arrangement suits a room with a suitable opening and enough internal clearance.

  • Check the clear space between opposing counters.
  • Keep the refrigerator near the entry end where practical.
  • Provide safe extraction and ventilation for cooking.
Planning note: Kitchen diagrams are illustrative. Finalize appliance sizes, ventilation, gas/electrical safety, water supply, drainage and counter heights with qualified local professionals.
