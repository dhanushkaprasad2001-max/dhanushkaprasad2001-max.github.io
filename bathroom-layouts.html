Room Layout Ideas

Bathroom Layouts

Explore compact 5×7 ft, 6×8 ft and 8×8 ft bathroom layouts with shower, toilet, vanity, access and ventilation guidance.

Bathroom layouts by footprint

Plan the door swing, shower screen, toilet clearance, vanity storage, ventilation and waterproofing before choosing finishes.

Concept 01 · 5 × 7 ft

Compact Three-Fixture Bathroom

A compact shower, toilet and narrow vanity are arranged along the two sides to keep the entrance usable.

  • Use a sliding or outward-opening door if allowed and safe.
  • Choose a compact vanity and shower screen.
  • Provide mechanical or natural ventilation.
Concept 02 · 6 × 8 ft

Comfortable Family Bathroom

A larger shower zone and wider vanity improve everyday usability while keeping the toilet away from the direct entry view.

  • Use slip-resistant floor finishes.
  • Keep the shower floor correctly graded to the drain.
  • Include accessible towel and storage locations.
Concept 03 · 8 × 8 ft

Bathroom with Separate Bath and Shower

A more spacious arrangement can accommodate a separate shower and bathtub with a dedicated vanity zone.

  • Check clearances around the tub and shower screen.
  • Plan waterproofing and drainage carefully.
  • Provide suitable ventilation and lighting.
Planning note: Bathroom diagrams are illustrative only. Have a qualified professional confirm plumbing, drainage falls, waterproofing, electrical safety zones and local code requirements.
