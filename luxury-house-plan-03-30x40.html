Luxury House Plan 03: A Practical Guide to Reviewing This House Plan

This concept image can help you discuss room relationships, circulation and the overall appearance with a design professional.

This concept presents a modern, single-storey white villa with extensive glazing, a stone feature wall and a long pool. The floor-plan panel labels three bedrooms, two bathrooms, a combined living/dining area and a kitchen.

Image review: The graphic states 30 ft × 40 ft and shows an infinity pool labelled 20 ft × 8 ft. The pool is drawn along the front edge of the plan, but no complete site boundary, setback dimensions, pool access details or structural section is supplied. The cutaway is a perspective illustration, not a measured confirmation of the 2D layout. Verify the bathroom positions and the relationship between the glazed façade and the pool before using the concept.

Planning the open living area

The living and dining spaces are combined to create a larger shared zone. This can improve the sense of space, but furniture placement should leave clear routes to bedrooms, the kitchen and the outdoor area. Check glare, heat gain and privacy where large glazed openings face the pool or neighbouring properties.

Kitchen and bedroom arrangement

The drawing labels three bedrooms and a kitchen. Confirm the actual room dimensions, wardrobe locations, door swings and window sizes. The kitchen needs a practical work triangle, adequate ventilation and safe separation between cooking appliances and circulation routes. Bathroom ventilation and waterproofing should be included in the technical design.

Pool and façade coordination

An infinity-edge pool requires specialist structural and hydraulic design, including the balancing tank, overflow channel, filtration equipment and safe maintenance access. Large glass panels also require appropriate safety glazing, shading and structural support. Do not assume these details are resolved by the visual.

Construction documents and costs

Have a qualified professional prepare the site plan, measured floor plan, elevations, sections, roof or slab details, structural drawings and service layouts. A cost plan should separate the main building, glazing, stone cladding, pool system, landscaping and external works. Use current local quotations and identify the estimate date and exclusions.

  • Confirm the 30 ft × 40 ft site and setbacks
  • Verify three bedrooms and two bathrooms on a dimensioned plan
  • Obtain pool engineering and water-management details
  • Specify glazing, shading, waterproofing and drainage
  • Complete approvals and a project-specific bill of quantities
