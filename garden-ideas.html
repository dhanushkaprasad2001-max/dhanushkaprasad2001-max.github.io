Small Garden Ideas

Begin by measuring the usable area and identifying sunny, shaded and wet spots. Use a simple path, a few planting groups and vertical growing space rather than filling every corner. Choose plants suited to the local climate and the amount of maintenance you can manage.

Front Yard Designs

Keep the route from gate to entrance clear and safe. Combine durable paving with planting beds, provide a place for rainwater to drain and avoid tall plants that block visibility near the driveway or gate.

Backyard Designs

Plan outdoor seating around shade, privacy and access from the kitchen or living room. Allow space for maintenance, clothes drying, water storage or other household needs before adding decorative features.

Plan before planting

Check soil, drainage, water availability, sun exposure, mature plant size and local conditions. Use non-invasive plants and keep tree roots and branches away from foundations, drains and overhead services. Images are inspiration; adapt every idea to the actual property.
