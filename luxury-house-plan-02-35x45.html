Luxury House Plan 02: Exploring the Home Layout and Design

Use the visual layout to consider how the rooms, circulation and exterior design could work together on a real site.

This illustration shows a white, single-storey villa with a brown tiled hip roof, a columned front veranda and a swimming pool. The 2D graphic labels four bedrooms and two bathrooms, with an open living, dining and kitchen zone.

Image review: The drawing labels the site as 35 ft × 45 ft and the pool as 15 ft × 20 ft, but it does not show a complete site plan with setbacks or a clear dimensioned building footprint. The cutaway and floor-plan panels are not sufficient to confirm that the pool, veranda and house all fit the stated site. Treat the bedroom and bathroom labels as provisional until checked on a coordinated plan.

Layout and circulation

The four bedrooms are arranged around a central shared living and dining space. This can keep family activity central while allowing bedrooms to sit at the outer edges. Before adopting the layout, check whether bedroom doors open into usable circulation space and whether bathroom doors provide privacy from the dining area. Confirm that the kitchen has practical worktop, storage and refrigerator space.

Pool and outdoor planning

A 15 ft × 20 ft pool is a substantial feature. Its location must be tested against the actual boundary, house footprint, access path, drainage and required safety separation. The image does not establish pool depth, construction type, filtration location or safety barrier design. These need separate drawings and professional review.

Exterior and roof considerations

The exterior concept uses a tiled hip roof and a deep veranda supported by columns. A roof plan should show ridge and valley locations, pitch, gutters, downpipes, flashing and rainwater discharge. The veranda columns and roof loads must be designed rather than copied from the illustration.

Before estimating or building

Confirm whether 35 ft × 45 ft is the land size or the building size. Obtain measured plans, elevations, sections, structural details and coordinated electrical and plumbing layouts. Price the house and pool separately using current local quotations, stating currency, date, finishes and exclusions.

  • Verified plot dimensions, setbacks and building footprint
  • Measured room dimensions and door/window positions
  • Roof, veranda and structural engineering details
  • Pool structure, filtration, drainage and safety design
  • Local approvals and itemised bill of quantities
