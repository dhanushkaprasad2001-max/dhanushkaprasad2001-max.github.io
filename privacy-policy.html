Privacy Policy

How DreamHome Plans handles information when you visit this website.

Last updated: November 8, 2026

Information you provide

If you use our contact form, the name, email address, subject and message you submit are sent to Formspree, our form-processing provider, so we can receive and respond to your enquiry. Formspree processes this information under its own privacy terms. Please do not submit passwords, payment details or other sensitive personal information.

How information is used

Information submitted through the contact form is used to review and respond to your enquiry and maintain communication about that enquiry.

Cookies, analytics and advertising

DreamHome Plans has added Google AdSense to this website. If ads are approved and served, Google and its advertising partners may display advertisements on the site. Google and its advertising partners may use cookies or similar technologies to store or access information on your browser, such as cookie identifiers, IP address and information about your visit, in order to provide, deliver, limit, personalize and measure advertisements. The use of these technologies may allow ads to be based on your visits to this website and other websites, depending on your settings, location and applicable consent requirements.

Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to this website or other websites. Learn more about how Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and Google's Privacy Policy.

Users can review or manage advertising personalization through Google My Ad Center. Users may also manage cookies through their browser settings. Disabling cookies may affect some advertising features.

For visitors in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, Google requires publishers to use a Google-certified consent management platform (CMP) when serving personalized ads, and to obtain consent where required by law. Before serving personalized ads to visitors in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, we will set up the applicable Google-certified consent management platform (CMP) through AdSense Privacy & messaging and obtain consent where required by law. See Google's consent management guidance.

DreamHome Plans does not currently use a separate third-party analytics service. If this changes, this policy will be updated.

Third-party services and external links

Some images are hosted by Unsplash, and contact-form submissions are processed by Formspree. The website may also link to other external websites. These third parties operate under their own privacy policies and terms. We do not control their data practices; please review their policies when using their services.

Advertising and data choices

Advertising services may collect or receive information as described above. The availability of ad personalization and consent options may vary by country and browser. You can use the controls provided by Google and your browser to manage available choices.

Data security and retention

Contact-form information is handled by Formspree and may be retained according to its settings and policies. Do not send confidential or sensitive information through the form.

Your choices

You can choose not to submit the contact form. You can also use your browser settings to manage or delete cookies and limit certain browser storage, although some third-party website features may not work as expected.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when website features or third-party services change. The latest revision date is shown above.

Contact

For privacy-related questions, please use our Contact Us page.
