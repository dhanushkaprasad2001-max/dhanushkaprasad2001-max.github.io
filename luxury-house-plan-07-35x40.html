Luxury House Plan 07: Evaluating the Home Layout Before Building

Study the plan and exterior together, then adapt the arrangement to the actual site, climate and local regulations.

This modern two-storey concept combines a covered two-car porch, four labelled bedrooms, a central living area and a rooftop terrace. The exterior illustration shows a flat-roofed contemporary form with a terrace above.

Image review: The heading gives 35 ft × 40 ft, and the floor-plan panel shows a 20 ft × 12 ft car porch and a 35 ft × 10 ft rooftop terrace. The plan labels two bathrooms, but the footer claims three. The cutaway does not clearly reconcile the four bedroom labels with the floor-by-floor arrangement. The porch and terrace dimensions also need to be checked against the actual site boundary and building footprint.

Organising the rooms

The open living room is positioned as the central shared space, with dining and kitchen areas nearby. Confirm the intended location of all four bedrooms on separate floor plans. Check whether the entry, stairs and bedroom doors provide clear circulation and privacy. The kitchen should include practical work surfaces, storage, ventilation and safe appliance clearances.

Car porch and rooftop terrace

A two-car porch needs verified vehicle dimensions, turning space, column positions and a safe pedestrian entrance. The rooftop terrace requires structural load calculations, waterproofing, parapet or guardrail design, drainage falls and safe stair access. Outdoor furniture and planters add loads that must be included in the design.

Flat roof, structure and services

Flat roofs still need carefully designed falls, outlets, overflow drainage and waterproofing. A structural engineer should design foundations, columns, beams and floor slabs. Coordinate bathrooms, plumbing stacks, electrical circuits, lighting, ventilation and rainwater pipes before construction.

Correcting the area and cost schedule

Do not assume 35 ft × 40 ft is total built-up area. Measure each enclosed floor separately and list the porch and rooftop terrace as separate areas. Resolve the two-versus-three bathroom discrepancy before preparing a bill of quantities. Use current local rates and identify currency, estimate date, finish level and exclusions.

  • Separate ground- and upper-floor plans
  • Confirm four bedrooms and the correct bathroom count
  • Check porch and rooftop terrace dimensions
  • Obtain flat-roof waterproofing and structural details
  • Prepare an area schedule, approvals and itemised budget
