Home Design Articles

Practical starting points for thinking about layouts, interiors and the process of planning a home.

Home design guide

How to Start Planning a New Home

Begin with your lifestyle, site conditions and realistic budget. List the rooms you need, how you move through them and which spaces should connect. Before finalizing a layout, check local planning rules, access, drainage, sunlight and ventilation with qualified professionals.

Home design guide

Five Things to Consider Before Choosing a House Layout

Think about privacy, circulation, natural light, storage and future flexibility. A layout should respond to the shape and orientation of the land, not just the appearance of a reference image. Ask a qualified designer to adapt ideas to your site and local requirements.

Home design guide

Creating a Calm and Comfortable Living Room

Start by identifying the room's main activity and the best focal point. Arrange seating to support conversation, leave clear walking paths and layer general, task and accent lighting. A restrained palette and a mix of textures can make a room feel warm without clutter.

Home design guide

Making a Small Bedroom Work Better

Measure the room and furniture before arranging anything. Keep the main walking route clear, use vertical storage where suitable and avoid blocking doors or windows. Choose furniture that fits the room rather than filling every available corner.

Home design guide

Planning a Practical Kitchen

Consider the relationship between preparation, cooking, washing and storage. Ensure work surfaces and circulation are comfortable for the people using the kitchen. Electrical, plumbing, ventilation and safety details should be planned with qualified professionals.

Home design guide

Why Natural Light Matters in Home Design

Daylight can make rooms feel more welcoming and reduce the need for artificial lighting during the day. Window size and placement should also consider privacy, glare, heat, weather and the local climate.
