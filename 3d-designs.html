What a useful 3D home design should show

A helpful 3D model is more than a decorative exterior image. It should be built from the same plan used to position rooms, doors and windows. A furnished top view helps review circulation and furniture footprints; an exterior view explains massing, roof form, shading and materials; and a roof or cutaway view can reveal relationships that are difficult to understand from a single perspective.

Review the plan before polishing the render

Start with a measured site and a coordinated 2D layout. Add wall thickness, openings, levels and roof geometry before applying materials and lighting. Compare the model with the plan and elevations so that each window, door and room appears in the same place. Use real product dimensions for furniture and joinery. Ask qualified local professionals to resolve structural, electrical, plumbing and regulatory decisions.
