Luxury House Plan 06: House Plan Design Review and Planning Checklist

Use this visual reference to prepare questions about room sizes, access, daylight, ventilation and construction details.

This single-storey villa concept uses a red clay tiled roof, a deep front veranda and four labelled bedrooms. The floor-plan graphic also labels a living/dining area and kitchen, with the veranda extending across the front.

Image review: The graphic states 30 ft × 45 ft and a total area of 1,350 sq ft, which is the simple product of those two dimensions. It is not proof of enclosed built-up area, especially because the veranda is shown as an additional zone. The bathroom labels and fixtures are not consistently clear between the 2D and cutaway panels. Confirm the number and position of bathrooms, and whether 30 ft × 45 ft refers to the site or building footprint.

Veranda and family spaces

The long covered veranda can provide shade and a sheltered transition between the garden and the interior. Check column spacing, entry steps, wheelchair access where required and rainwater runoff. Inside, the living/dining area connects to the kitchen, so allow enough space for a dining table, food preparation and circulation without blocking bedroom access.

Bedroom privacy and ventilation

The plan labels four bedrooms around the shared area. Verify each room's dimensions, wardrobe position, window size and door swing on a measured drawing. Ensure bedrooms have suitable daylight and cross-ventilation where possible. Confirm the bathroom count and provide mechanical or natural ventilation as required.

Roof and construction details

The exterior suggests a pitched tiled roof. A proper roof plan must identify ridge lines, hips, valleys, pitch, underlay, flashing, gutters and downpipes. The veranda columns and roof framing need engineering for local wind and weather conditions. Confirm termite protection, damp-proofing and foundation design for the site soil.

Budget and next steps

Separate enclosed floor area from veranda and other covered spaces in the area schedule. Price roof tiles, roof framing, columns, finishes, doors, windows, plumbing and electrical work from current local quotations. Include drainage, landscaping, approvals and professional fees as separate items.

  • Verified plot and building dimensions
  • Four-bedroom plan with confirmed bathroom locations
  • Veranda, column and roof-framing details
  • Window, door, electrical and plumbing schedules
  • Local approvals and an itemised bill of quantities
