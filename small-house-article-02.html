22 × 28-Foot Modern Two-Bedroom Small House Plan

Compact Floor-Plan Concept, Furniture Arrangement, Exterior Inspiration and Complete Planning Guide

A comfortable home does not always need a large footprint. This 22 × 28-foot concept explores a compact single-storey arrangement with two bedrooms, one bathroom, a living and dining area, a kitchen and a small front porch. It is an inspiration guide for households planning a modest home on a compact plot.

1. Design Overview

The design aims to keep shared family spaces close to the entrance and place bedrooms in a quieter zone. The final arrangement must respond to the actual plot, orientation, setbacks, access and local regulations.

3. 2D Floor-Plan Arrangement

Consider placing both bedrooms along the quieter side or rear of the home, with a shared bathroom accessible from a short passage. The living area can sit near the main entrance, while the kitchen connects conveniently to dining. Keep circulation direct and avoid long corridors.

  • Show all external and internal dimensions on the final drawing.
  • Mark wall thicknesses, door swings and exact window locations.
  • Check that furniture does not obstruct doors or access to windows.
  • Confirm that bathroom and kitchen services have practical routes.

4. Bedroom Furniture Planning

For the main bedroom, test a double bed, wardrobe and bedside storage in the scaled plan. In the second bedroom, choose a single or compact double bed based on household needs. Sliding wardrobe doors or wall-mounted shelves may preserve floor clearance.

5. Living and Dining Area

A compact sofa, small coffee table and wall-mounted television can help keep the living zone open. If dining shares this space, consider a small two- or four-seat table and preserve a clear route between the entrance, kitchen and bedroom passage.

6. Kitchen Design

A straight or L-shaped counter may be suitable depending on the final room shape. Plan the sink, preparation surface, cooktop and refrigerator as a practical working sequence. Confirm appliance clearances, storage and ventilation before ordering cabinets.

7. Bathroom Planning

Arrange the toilet, washbasin and shower so the door opens freely and the wet area is easy to clean. Include waterproofing, suitable floor falls, drainage access and an operable privacy window or mechanical exhaust ventilation.

8. Doors, Windows and Ventilation

  • Place the main entrance from the porch into the living area.
  • Position bedroom doors to maintain privacy and useful furniture walls.
  • Provide external windows for daylight and cross-ventilation where possible.
  • Provide suitable extraction for kitchen fumes and bathroom moisture.
  • Check sun, rain, privacy and security when choosing openings.

9. Furnished 3D Layout

Use the 3D top view to check how beds, wardrobes, sofas, kitchen units and dining furniture fit. Produce the 3D model from the same dimensioned 2D drawing. Check walking clearances, door swings and access to windows; a decorative render alone cannot confirm dimensions.

10. Modern Exterior and Roof

A contemporary tropical exterior could combine light-coloured walls, a timber-look entrance feature, stone-texture accents, shaded windows and low-maintenance planting. A lightweight sloping roof is one possible option. Roof pitch, structure, covering, insulation, gutters and rainwater disposal must be designed for local rainfall and wind conditions.

11. Electrical and Plumbing Planning

Plan lighting, switches and socket outlets before plastering. Include practical points for bedside use, television, kitchen appliances, refrigerator and exterior lighting. Electrical installation must follow applicable local standards and be checked by a qualified professional.

Keep kitchen and bathroom water and drainage routes practical and maintainable. Confirm pipe routes, gradients, waterproofing, ventilation and access to shut-off valves before construction.

12. Construction Stages

  1. Survey the plot and confirm boundaries, levels, access and approval requirements.
  2. Assess ground conditions and obtain a suitable foundation design.
  3. Set out and construct the approved foundation.
  4. Complete floor preparation and structural work.
  5. Build walls and install designed structural elements.
  6. Install the roof frame, covering and rainwater system.
  7. Complete first-fix electrical and plumbing work.
  8. Apply plaster, waterproofing and protective finishes.
  9. Install doors, windows, flooring, ceilings and fittings.
  10. Complete painting, external works, testing and final inspection.

13. Materials and Budget Checklist

Costs vary with location, soil, design, material specifications, labour rates and market conditions. Prepare a site-specific estimate using final measured drawings and current local quotations.

  • Site preparation and foundation
  • Concrete, reinforcement and structural work
  • Blocks or bricks, mortar and plaster
  • Roof frame, covering, insulation and gutters
  • Doors, windows and hardware
  • Electrical wiring, fittings and testing
  • Water supply, drainage and bathroom fixtures
  • Kitchen cabinets and worktops
  • Floor finishes, paint and ceiling finishes
  • Labour, transport, approvals and contingency

14. Is This Concept Right for Your Land?

This design direction may suit a small household seeking two bedrooms and compact shared spaces. Confirm that the site can accommodate the building, setbacks, access, drainage and any required parking or outdoor service areas. Obtain locally compliant architectural, structural, electrical and plumbing documents before building.

Conclusion

The 22 × 28-foot modern two-bedroom concept offers inspiration for a compact family home with a simple exterior and carefully planned everyday spaces. Turn the idea into a coordinated, accurately measured drawing that fits the actual land and local building requirements before construction.
