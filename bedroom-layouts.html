Concept 01 · 10 × 10 ft

Compact Bedroom with Study Desk

A double bed sits along the left side, with a wardrobe on the upper-right wall and a compact desk near the lower-right corner.

  • Keep the door swing clear.
  • Use a sliding wardrobe door where space is tight.
  • Place the desk near natural light where possible.
Concept 02 · 10 × 12 ft

Balanced Queen-Bed Layout

A centered queen bed with matching bedside tables creates a calm, symmetrical arrangement. A dresser and wardrobe use the foot-end zone.

  • Allow a comfortable route on both sides of the bed.
  • Keep drawers and wardrobe doors operable.
  • Use wall-mounted lighting to free bedside surface space.
Concept 03 · 12 × 13 ft

Bedroom with Separate Vanity Zone

A larger room can accommodate a king bed, full-height storage and a dedicated dressing table without crowding the central route.

  • Keep a clear approach to the bed and windows.
  • Check wardrobe depth and door type.
  • Plan power outlets for bedside and vanity use.
Planning note: These are illustrative concepts, not construction drawings. Confirm actual wall thicknesses, window sizes, door swings, electrical points and furniture dimensions before purchasing or building.
