Luxury House Plan 08: A Guide to the Floor Plan and Exterior Views

This visual guide is a starting point for discussing layout, privacy, natural light and the home's external appearance.

This image presents a white villa with a grey tiled hip roof, a formal entrance and a circular fountain. The floor-plan graphic labels three bedrooms and several bathrooms, with living and dining areas arranged around the entrance.

Image review: The title gives 30 ft × 40 ft, but the 2D plan and cutaway do not show a consistent bathroom arrangement. The cutaway includes repeated bathroom labels and room relationships that are difficult to reconcile with the plan. The fountain is shown at the entry, but there is no site plan demonstrating its clearances, drainage or access. Treat the three-bedroom count as a visual label only and verify all room and bathroom details.

Entrance and shared living

The central entrance and fountain create a strong arrival feature. Keep a clear, non-slip route from the gate to the front door and ensure the fountain does not obstruct circulation. The living and dining zones should have enough space for furniture while maintaining direct routes to the kitchen and bedrooms.

Bedroom and bathroom planning

The drawing labels three bedrooms. Confirm each room's measured dimensions, wardrobes, windows and door swings. Resolve the exact bathroom count and fixture arrangement on a clean plan. Bathrooms need waterproofing, floor falls, ventilation, accessible service routes and privacy from living and dining spaces.

Roof and exterior details

The exterior suggests a tiled hip roof. Ask for a roof plan showing pitch, hips, valleys, gutters, downpipes and rainwater discharge. Verify window placement, shading, wall finishes and foundation levels against the actual site and climate. The entrance feature and any stone or decorative finish should be included in the specification.

Site, services and budget

Confirm whether 30 ft × 40 ft is the plot or building dimension. Locate the fountain, drainage, water supply and electrical points on the site plan. Obtain structural, electrical and plumbing drawings before construction. Estimate costs only from verified areas and specifications, using local quotations and clearly stated exclusions.

  • Dimensioned three-bedroom floor plan
  • Correct bathroom count and fixture schedule
  • Fountain location, water supply and drainage plan
  • Roof plan, elevations and structural details
  • Approvals and project-specific bill of quantities
