Living Room Layouts

Explore distinct small living room arrangements with practical seating, TV placement and clear routes between opposite doors.

Different ways to arrange a small living room

These concepts prioritize circulation, clear door approaches and varied furniture arrangements rather than repeating the same sofa-and-TV positions.

Concept 01 · 10 × 10 ft

Opposite-Door Passage Layout

A compact sofa is placed on the top wall, the TV unit is opposite it, and the left/right door approaches are kept open with a clear route through the room.

  • Use slim furniture with shallow depth.
  • Keep the center route free of bulky tables.
  • Confirm both door swings before final placement.
Concept 02 · 10 × 10 ft

Sofa-and-Two-Chair Layout

A long sofa along one side and two separate chairs opposite create a conversation zone while the center remains open for movement between the two entrances.

  • Choose compact accent chairs.
  • Avoid a large central coffee table.
  • Use small side tables beside seating.
Concept 03 · 10 × 12 ft

Corner Sectional Layout

An L-shaped sofa uses one corner efficiently, with the TV on the short wall and a compact occasional chair. Keep the entrance zone clear.

  • Use a small movable ottoman instead of a fixed table.
  • Keep the route to windows and doors unobstructed.
  • Check viewing distance before mounting the TV.
Planning note: The diagrams are concept sketches and not to scale. For two-door rooms, check the actual door leaf width, swing arcs and a comfortable clear passage before fixing furniture positions.
