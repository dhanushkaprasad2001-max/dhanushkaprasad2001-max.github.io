Luxury House Plan 09: Reviewing the Home Design Concept

Consider how the illustrated spaces connect and what changes may be needed for your plot and household.

This concept shows a modern white villa arranged around a central courtyard and a small pool. The 2D plan labels a 40 ft × 40 ft layout, several bedrooms and a courtyard, while the cutaway illustrates the indoor-outdoor relationship.

Image review: The floor-plan panel appears to label “Bedroom 3” twice, although the heading and footer claim five bedrooms. The cutaway labels bedrooms differently and does not clearly confirm the same count. The bathroom count also differs between the plan and footer. The stated 40 ft × 40 ft dimension and 1,600 sq ft figure describe a simple square area, not verified enclosed built-up area. The courtyard and pool need a measured site and drainage plan.

Courtyard concept

A central courtyard can bring daylight and ventilation into surrounding rooms and create a private outdoor space. Confirm whether the feature is an open courtyard, a pool or both; the drawing uses these ideas inconsistently. Provide falls, waterproofing, overflow drainage, safe walking surfaces and maintenance access. Protect adjacent rooms from water ingress.

Bedroom zoning and privacy

The concept intends to provide multiple bedrooms around the central space. A revised plan should number each bedroom once, identify the master suite and show the correct bathroom count. Check door swings, wardrobe locations, window positions and acoustic privacy. Make sure the living/dining space remains connected without becoming a narrow passage.

Roof, structure and daylight

The exterior suggests a flat-roof contemporary villa. A roof plan should show drainage falls, outlets, waterproofing and access for maintenance. Courtyard openings may affect structural spans and weather protection, so the structural engineer must coordinate beams, columns and foundations with the architectural plan.

Budget and documentation

Calculate the enclosed building area separately from the courtyard and pool. Obtain measured drawings, elevations, sections, structural details and coordinated services. Price the courtyard finishes, pool equipment, waterproofing, glazing and landscaping separately from the main house using current local quotations.

  • Corrected bedroom numbering and bathroom schedule
  • Verified 40 ft × 40 ft site and building dimensions
  • Courtyard/pool drainage and waterproofing details
  • Roof, structural, electrical and plumbing drawings
  • Local approvals and an itemised cost estimate
