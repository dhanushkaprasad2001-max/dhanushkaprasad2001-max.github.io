Luxury House Plan 05: Reviewing the Interior Arrangement and Exterior

Compare the floor-plan presentation with the exterior and interior views before deciding which design ideas suit your needs.

This concept shows a modern white two-storey villa with a glass balcony and a swimming pool. The upper-right panel is labelled as a first-floor plan, while its drawing also includes living and kitchen spaces, stairs and a pool. The lower-left cutaway depicts two levels.

Image review: The image title says 30 ft × 40 ft, but the plan panel is labelled “first floor plan” and also “ground floor plan” near its dimension line. It shows four bedroom labels and a pool, while the cutaway depicts a two-storey arrangement. The footer's 1,200 sq ft figure is simply 30 × 40 and must not be treated as verified total built-up area for a two-storey home. The drawing set is internally inconsistent and requires replacement with separate floor plans.

Separate the two floor layouts

Before developing this concept, prepare one ground-floor plan and one first-floor plan. Each must show the floor boundary, room names, dimensions, stairs, openings and circulation. Confirm which bedrooms belong on each level and whether the living room and kitchen are on the intended floor. Do not count the same space twice.

Balcony, pool and outdoor safety

The glass balcony needs engineered supports, safe balustrade heights and suitable safety glazing. The pool requires a site plan, structural design, filtration and drainage details, slip-resistant surrounding finishes and appropriate child-safety measures. Confirm the pool can fit without violating setbacks or blocking access.

Structure and building services

Have an engineer design foundations, columns, beams, suspended slabs and stairs for the actual site. Coordinate bathrooms vertically where possible to simplify plumbing, while ensuring ventilation and access for maintenance. Plan electrical circuits, lighting, air-conditioning routes, water supply and wastewater before fixing the room arrangement.

Accurate area and budget

Calculate ground-floor and upper-floor areas separately, then state the total built-up area using the measurement convention required locally. Keep the pool and external areas separate from enclosed floor area. Prepare a bill of quantities from approved drawings and current local rates, with the currency, date and exclusions clearly stated.

  • Correctly labelled ground- and first-floor plans
  • Floor-by-floor area schedule and verified room count
  • Balcony, pool and waterproofing details
  • Structural, electrical and plumbing drawings
  • Site approvals and a measured cost estimate
