How to plan an interior that works every day

Start with measurements and the activities that must happen in the room. Draw furniture to scale before choosing colours or buying accessories. Check the route through the space with doors open and chairs pulled out, then coordinate lighting, power, data, ventilation and storage. Select finishes using physical samples in the room's real light.

For connected spaces, compare the interior with the adjoining kitchen layouts and living room layouts. For complete-home context, browse Modern Homes and House Plans.
